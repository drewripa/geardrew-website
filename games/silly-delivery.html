Silly Delivery

An isometric voxel driving game. You start as the paper kid on a bike in Maple Hollow, a made-up small American town, and work your way up to running a delivery business.

In development AndroidPCMac

What you do

  • Pick up paper routes and throw newspapers onto porches. Later, with a phone, deliver food from the diner, the burger joint and the grocery stores.
  • Watch your energy. Grab a coffee or a meal at Rosie's Diner, or eat Mom's sandwich on a park bench.
  • Spend your money and the town grows. New streets go up and new shops open.
  • Upgrade your bike and dress the kid at the town's clothes, shoe, backpack and watch shops.
  • The town lives its own life: buses on four lines, a mail truck on its rounds, farm tractors, pets in the yards and townsfolk chatting outside the shops.
  • Find collectibles: little voxel trophies that you keep for good.
  • Easy mode (an arrow shows the way) or Normal mode (find it on the map yourself).
  • In English, Ukrainian and Spanish.
