Silly Delivery

Ride, deliver and watch your town grow! A cozy voxel delivery game for kids. Start as the paper kid on a bike in Maple Hollow, a made-up small American town, and work your way up the delivery ladder.

In development AndroidPCMac

Screenshots

What you do

  • Pick up paper routes and throw newspapers onto porches. Later, with a phone, deliver food from the diner, the burger joint and the grocery stores, with tips for quick riders.
  • Choose your path: on day 5 D-Livery calls with a job. Say yes and drive a different vehicle from the company fleet each day, throwing parcels onto the porches. Say no and stay a freelance courier on two wheels, saving up for one of Wheelie World's 10 bikes and, one day, a car.
  • Watch your energy. Grab a coffee or a meal at Rosie's Diner, or eat Mom's sandwich on a park bench.
  • Spend your money and the town grows. New streets go up and new shops open.
  • Upgrade your bike and dress the kid at the town's clothes, shoe, backpack and watch shops.
  • The town lives its own life: buses on four lines, a mail truck on its rounds, fire engines, farm tractors, pets in the yards and townsfolk chatting outside the shops.
  • Sunny days, rain, fog and thunderstorms; at dusk the street lamps and windows light up one by one.
  • Find collectibles: little voxel trophies behind silly challenges, kept for good. One is legendary.
  • Made for kids and families: no violence, no ads, no internet needed. Touch, keyboard or controller.
  • Easy mode (an arrow shows the way) or Normal mode (find it on the map yourself).
  • In English, Ukrainian and Spanish.
