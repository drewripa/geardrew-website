Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy covers the games published by GearDrew ("we", "us") on Google Play and other stores. It explains what information our games handle and why.

Summary

Our games do not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

Silly Delivery

Information we collect

None. Silly Delivery does not ask for any personal information and does not send any data over the internet. It does not request any special device permissions.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, settings (such as language and sound) and collectibles in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Third parties

The game contains no ads, analytics or third-party SDKs that collect data. If you download it from Google Play, Google's own privacy policy covers the store itself.

Realm of the Night

Realm of the Night follows the same rules: no personal information is collected or shared, and any progress is saved only on your device.

Children's privacy

Our games are suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, we collect none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Changes to this policy

If a game starts handling data in a new way, we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
