Silly Delivery Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

This privacy policy applies to Silly Delivery (the "app"), a game made and published by GearDrew ("we", "us", "our"), including its full version and its free demo, on Android (Google Play), Windows and macOS. It explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and who it is shared with.

Summary

  • We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data.
  • The app does not connect to the internet and sends no data to us or anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.
  • We do not share or sell any data with any third parties.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect or receive any personal information, such as your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers or advertising ID. It does not use the camera, microphone or location.

Device permissions

The Android app requests no device permissions, not even internet access.

Data stored on your device

To work as a game, the app keeps a few files in its own private storage on your device:

  • Game progress: your town, money, purchases in the game's shops and the current day.
  • Settings: language, graphics, map and control preferences, and whether the tutorial has been shown.
  • Collectibles: which collectibles you have found, with the date and time you found each one.

The app reads your device's language setting to choose the game's language. It doesn't store or send this setting anywhere.

These files never leave your device. They are not sent to us, are not shared with anyone, and are excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup. You can delete them at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

How we use information

The files above are used only by the app itself, on your device, to save your game and your preferences. We have no access to them.

Sharing of information

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any user data with any third parties, because the app collects none. The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social or tracking libraries (SDKs).

App stores

If you download the app from Google Play, Google handles the download, installation and updates under its own privacy policy. We do not receive any personal information about you from Google beyond the anonymous, aggregated statistics Google provides to all developers (such as the number of installs).

Data security and retention

As we collect and store no user data on our side, there is nothing for us to retain, secure or delete. Your on-device game files stay until you delete them or uninstall the app.

Children's privacy

Silly Delivery is made for players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and the app has no way to do so. The app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and Google Play's Families policies. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us at the address below.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about players of this app. If you have any request or question, contact us and we will reply.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles data, we will update this page and its dates before that version is released, and the app's store listing will link to the updated policy.
