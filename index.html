About

GearDrew is an independent game studio. We build small, warm worlds out of voxels: towns full of little stories that you can play in short sessions on your phone or sink an evening into on PC.

Every GearDrew game uses Geardrew Pixel, our own pixel font, drawn by hand to look the same in English, Spanish and Ukrainian.
